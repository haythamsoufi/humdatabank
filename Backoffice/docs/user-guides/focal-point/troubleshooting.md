# Troubleshooting

Use this page for quick fixes when something is not working. Try the [first checks](#first-checks) before working through a specific issue below.

## First checks

- Refresh the page.
- Log out and log back in.
- Try another browser (if possible).
- If you are on a slow connection, wait a few seconds after clicking buttons before closing the tab.

## No assignments appearing

**Possible causes:**

- You are not assigned to any country yet.
- There are no active assignments for your country/countries.

**Solution:**

- Confirm you can see your country/countries (if your organization uses country access).
- Contact your administrator and ask them to confirm your focal point role, your country assignments, and that an assignment exists for your country.

## Can't submit the form

**Possible causes:**

- A required field is missing.
- A validation message is shown (for example, an invalid number format).
- The assignment is locked because it is already submitted or approved.

**Solution:**

- Scroll through all sections and look for highlighted fields or messages.
- Fill in missing required fields (marked with * if shown).
- Fix validation messages, then try **Submit** again.

## Submit button does nothing

**Possible causes:**

- A required field is missing in another section.
- A validation error is not visible without scrolling.

**Solution:**

- Scroll to the top and bottom to look for red or amber validation messages.
- Open each section that shows a warning and fix missing fields.
- Refresh the page once and try again.
- If it still fails, contact your administrator with the assignment name, the section you were in, and a screenshot of any message.

## Required fields missing but can't find them

**Possible causes:**

- The required field is in another section.
- A matrix or table has a required cell.
- The field appears only after a previous answer (conditional logic).

**Solution:**

- Use the section navigation and open each section that shows a warning.
- Look for fields marked with an asterisk (*) or highlighted borders.

## Progress missing after leaving the form

**Possible causes:**

- The page was closed before auto-save completed.
- A connection issue interrupted saving.

**Solution:**

- If you see **Save draft**, use it before leaving the page.
- Wait a few seconds after major edits before closing the tab.
- If work is still missing, contact support with the assignment name, when you last saw the data, and which sections were affected.

## Buttons missing (Edit, Submit, or Approve)

**Possible causes:**

- The submission status locks editing after submit or approval.
- Your role does not include that action.

**Solution:**

- Check [Submission Status](../common/submission-statuses-and-permissions.md).
- Contact your administrator to confirm your role and permissions.

## Can't edit after submitting

**Possible causes:**

- Editing is locked after submission until an administrator reopens the assignment.

**Solution:**

- This is usually expected. See [Submission Status](../common/submission-statuses-and-permissions.md).
- Contact your administrator if you need the assignment reopened.

## Assignment disappeared

**Possible causes:**

- The assignment is no longer active (closed).
- Your country access changed.
- You were removed from assignment roles.

**Solution:**

- Check your dashboard and filters (if available).
- Ask your administrator to confirm your country access, your data-entry role, and whether the assignment is still active.

## Access denied

**Possible causes:**

- Your role does not allow that page or action.
- You do not have access to the country or organization for that assignment.

**Solution:**

- Contact your administrator and share the page you tried to open, the assignment name (if relevant), and a screenshot of the message.

## Supporting document upload fails

**Possible causes:**

- The file is too large or the file type is not allowed.
- Your role does not allow document upload.
- The file name contains special characters.

**Solution:**

- Reduce the file size or compress images.
- Rename the file using simple letters and numbers.
- Try a different browser.
- See [Supporting documents (Focal point)](../../data-reporting/supporting-documents.md).
- If it still fails, share the file name, type, size, and assignment name with support.
