# Unified Planning and Reporting (UPR)

UPR is the Unified Planning and Reporting process: National Societies set objectives and targets, then report against them on a Federation-wide basis.

This plugin adds live dashboards, Excel import/export, GO-API document intelligence, and AI assistance for Unified Plans and Reports.

## What this plugin provides

- **Assignment visuals** — live dashboards for Unified Plan and Unified Report assignments (PDF, PNG, and InDesign export)
- **Excel import** — load UPR Master workbooks into planning and reporting templates from [UPR Tools](/admin/upr-tools#excel-import)
- **Guidance documents** — upload official UPR guidance so it can be imported into the [AI Knowledge Base](/admin/ai/knowledge-base)
- **AI assistant** — the chatbot can act as the Unified Planning and Reporting Assistant when UPR sources are enabled

## Reporting cycle

| Cycle | Period |
|---|---|
| Mid-year progress report | January – June |
| Annual report | January – December |

Plans often cover a three-year horizon (for example a 2025 plan covering 2025–2027).
