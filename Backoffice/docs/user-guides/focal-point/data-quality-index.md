# Data Quality Index (focal point)

The **Data Quality Index** (QoD) shows how complete and reliable your reported data is for an assignment. When your organization enables it, you will see a **Quality of Data** tab on the dashboard for each eligible template.

## What you see on the dashboard

- **Overall score** — a percentage from 0% to 100%.
- **Five pillars** — documents, reporting, disaggregation, timeliness, and validation questions.
- **Trend** — how the score changed across recent reporting periods (when history exists).

Scores are calculated automatically from your submitted form data and uploaded documents. They do not replace formal review by your National Society or IFRC.

## The five pillars (FDRS profile)

When your template uses the **FDRS v1** methodology, the overall score combines:

| Pillar | Weight | What it measures |
|--------|--------|------------------|
| Documents | 20% | Annual Report and Audited Financial Statement uploads |
| Reporting | 30% | Governance, finance, and people-reached indicators reported |
| Disaggregation | 30% | Sex, age, and disability-related breakdowns where applicable |
| Timeliness | 10% | Whether sections were submitted before the annual cutoff |
| Validation questions | 10% | Your responses to automatic data checks |

Overall = 20% documents + 30% reporting + 30% disaggregation + 10% timeliness + 10% validation questions.

If no validation questions were sent for your assignment, the validation pillar counts as fully complete (100%).

## Validation questions

Automatic checks may flag values that look unusual compared to thresholds, prior years, or reference data. When questions are sent:

1. You receive an **email** and/or **in-app notification**.
2. An **amber banner** appears at the top of the assignment form.
3. Open each question, review the evidence shown, and submit a short explanation.

Answering validation questions improves the **validation questions** pillar. Unanswered questions lower that part of your score.

### Tips for answering

- Explain *why* the value is correct (source, methodology, one-off event).
- If the value is wrong, correct the form field and mention that in your answer.
- Use the same language you normally use for reporting when possible.
